Human Cardiovascular System: The Heart

The heart is the primary organ responsible for circulating oxygenated and deoxygenated blood throughout the human body. Situated in the middle mediastinum of the thoracic cavity, it functions as a dual muscular pump operating through coordinated electrical and mechanical phases.

Key Anatomical Substructures:

  • Right & Left Atria: Receiving chambers that collect systemic venous blood (via superior/inferior vena cava) and pulmonary venous blood.
  • Right & Left Ventricles: High-pressure muscular chambers responsible for pulmonary and systemic ejection.
  • Coronary Circulation: Anterior interventricular and circumflex branches delivering oxygen to cardiac myocardium.
  • Atrioventricular & Semilunar Valves: Tricuspid, bicuspid (mitral), aortic, and pulmonary valves preventing regurgitant backflow.

Hemodynamic Principles:
